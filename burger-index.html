burgers

Here is the description about the steps it take
to produce this amazing burger

Ingredients

  • seasoned ground beef patties
  • toasted buns
  • melted cheese
  • fresh vegetables and
  • simple condiments

steps

  1. Portion and Shape: Preheat your oven to 400F (204C).
  2. Season: Divide your ground beef (80/20 lean-to-fat ratio) into loose balls, then press them into patties that are
    slightly wider than your buns. Make a small dimple in the center to keep them flat while cooking.
  3. Sear: Right before cooking, season both sides generously with salt and black pepper.
  4. Add Cheese::Place a slice of cheese (such as American or cheddar) on top of each patty during the last minute of cooking so it melts.
  5. Toast and Assemble:Lightly toast your burger buns in the pan or with a touch of butter/fat. Layer the bottom
    bun with sauce or lettuce, add the cheesy patty, top with your favorite condiments, and cover with the top bun.
